BAKA!! Trap Dojo — Privacy Policy

PaperLantern · Effective 18 October 2026

BAKA!! Trap Dojo ("the game") is made by PaperLantern. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play. The short version: nothing leaves your device.

What we collect

Nothing. The game has no accounts, no sign-up, no advertising, no analytics and no crash reporting. It does not send any data to us or to anyone else.

What is stored on your device

To remember your progress, the game saves the following on your device only:

  • which stages you have unlocked and cleared, and your best times;
  • how many times you have died, and where (to draw the skull markers);
  • how many times you have quit a stage halfway (so the sensei can tease you about it);
  • your sound and music settings.

This data never leaves your device. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's storage in your device settings or by uninstalling the game.

Permissions

The game uses vibration for haptic feedback. It does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. Because we collect no personal information from anyone, we collect none from children.

Changes

If a future version adds features that change this (for example, ads or purchases), we will update this policy and the effective date above before that version is released.
